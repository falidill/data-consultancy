If reporting takes all week and still does not answer the question, the system needs fixing.

Most growing teams do not have a data problem. They have a process and reporting problem. These are the patterns our team is hired to fix.

1

Reports are rebuilt by hand every week

Someone copies, pastes, and fixes formulas in Excel for hours. When that person is out, reporting stops or goes out wrong.

2

Your systems do not agree with each other

Client, billing, and operations data live in different places. Reconciliation takes days and no one is sure which number is right.

3

Leadership has no single view of performance

There is no weekly dashboard for the KPIs that matter, so decisions wait for the next report or happen without the full picture.

4

Follow ups slip through the cracks

Authorizations expire, partner records go stale, and volunteer or client follow ups are missed because there is no tracking and alert process.

Common questions

Before you email

Does ClearOps Consulting work remotely or on site?

We are based in New Haven, CT, and we work remotely with businesses. Discovery calls and working sessions are held by video or phone, and deliverables are shared digitally with clear documentation.

What systems does your team work with?

Our team works with Advanced Excel including VBA, XLOOKUP, and macros, plus SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Python with Pandas and NumPy, Alteryx, Salesforce, and Airtable. If your data lives in one of those, we can usually build reporting directly from it.

What happens on the discovery call?

It is a free 30 minute conversation with our team about your current reporting and operations pain points. We will ask what breaks most often, what leadership wishes they could see, and what systems you use. If we can help, we will outline next steps. If we cannot, we will say so.

Can ClearOps Consulting help if we only have Excel right now?

Yes. Many of our strongest results started in Excel. A well structured Excel control system with proper tracking, formulas, and follow up processes can fix a lot before you invest in new software.

Can you help with a one off data entry project?

Yes, from a single day to a year long engagement. We have people who can do data entry work for one off cleanups, backlog clearing, data migration entry, and ongoing records entry and updating, including spreadsheet and CRM entry in Salesforce and Airtable. Tell us the volume and deadline and we will staff it.
