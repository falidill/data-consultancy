Where we can help

When the day-to-day work is complex, reporting should make it simpler.

The challenge is often not a lack of data. It is information spread across files, manual steps, and processes that are difficult to maintain.

1

Reports are rebuilt by hand every week

Someone copies, pastes, and fixes formulas in Excel for hours. When that person is out, reporting stops or goes out wrong.

2

Your systems do not agree with each other

Client, billing, and operations data live in different places. Reconciliation takes days and no one is sure which number is right.

3

Leadership has no single view of performance

There is no weekly dashboard for the KPIs that matter, so decisions wait for the next report or happen without the full picture.

4

Follow ups slip through the cracks

Authorizations expire, partner records go stale, and volunteer or client follow ups are missed because there is no tracking and alert process.

What ClearOps does

Practical services built around the work you need done.

For organizations that need consistent reporting and informed follow-through, but do not need a full-time analytics position.

Targeted help getting records organized, updated, and ready to use. Work is sized to the volume, timeline, and available capacity.

Common questions

Before you email

Does ClearOps work remotely or on site?

Projects are generally delivered remotely, with working sessions by video or phone and secure exchange of agreed deliverables. Any on-site requirements are discussed individually.

What systems does yClearOps work with?

Depending on the project, ClearOps works with Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, SQL, Python, Salesforce, and Airtable. The approach starts with the tools an organization already has.

What happens on the discovery call?

The introductory call focuses on what is taking too much time, which systems are involved, and what a useful result would look like. If there is a fit, the next step is a scoped proposal. You do not need to share sensitive records on the call.

Can ClearOps Consulting help if we only have Excel right now?

Yes. Many of our strongest results started in Excel. A well structured Excel control system with proper tracking, formulas, and follow up processes can fix a lot before you invest in new software.

Can you help with a one off data entry project?

Yes, when the size and timeline are manageable. Share the type of records, estimated volume, required checks, and deadline to discuss a defined project. ClearOps does not promise unlimited staffing capacity.

How do we start?

Email bnumah.works@gmail.com with a short description of your organization, the reporting or workflow problem you want solved, and a couple of times that might work for a conversation. Please do not include confidential client or patient information in the initial email.

How is confidential business or healthcare data handled?

Data access, confidentiality, security expectations, and any applicable agreements are discussed before work begins. Healthcare engagements involving protected health information require an appropriate compliance arrangement, including a business associate agreement when applicable. Please do not send sensitive records through the initial contact email.
